Privacy Policy

The Scholar's Journal · Last updated: October 9, 2026

The Scholar's Journal ("the app") is developed by Nightpress Studios ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens with your information when you use the app. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information the app stores

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your device. This includes your journal entries and moods, study, meditation and exercise records, pacts, seals, levels and settings. The app has no user accounts and no servers, and I have no access to any of this information.

Information the app collects

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking tools and no third-party data collection SDKs. It does not make network requests to send your data anywhere.

Export and backup

The app lets you export your data as a code or a file so you can move it to another device. An export happens only when you start it, and the data goes only to the destination you choose (for example a file on your device, or an app you select in the Android share menu). What happens to that copy afterwards is governed by the service you sent it to.

The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes everything stored in it, so keep an exported copy if you want to preserve your data.

Permissions

Notifications: used only to tell you when a study timer ends while the app is in the background. Notifications are created on your device; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in Android settings.

Internet access: required by the framework the app is built with. The app works fully offline and does not use the connection to send your data.

External links

The app can open the Google Play page to rate it and your email app to send feedback. These actions happen only when you tap them. If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use them only to reply.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Wellbeing notice

The app is a themed self-improvement tool. Its "sanity" meter and other story elements are fiction and are not medical or psychological advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app adds features that involve data collection, such as purchases, ads or cloud sync, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
